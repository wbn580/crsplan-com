## Understanding the Need for Correction

When a cross-border resident discovers that an offshore financial account was omitted from a Common Reporting Standard (CRS) filing, prompt action is essential. The correction process may require voluntary disclosure and a formal amendment to the filed return.

## Steps for Voluntary Disclosure and Amendment

The first practical step is to review the original CRS filing to identify the specific account and the data that was missed. After that, the account holder or their authorised representative should prepare the corrected information and submit it through the appropriate channel. Some jurisdictions provide a voluntary disclosure programme, while others may require a direct amendment to the previous filing. Confirming the exact procedure with the relevant tax authority is recommended to ensure that the correction is accepted.

## Potential Penalties and Timelines

Penalties for failing to report an offshore account can vary by jurisdiction. In some places, there are set fine amounts, while in others the penalty depends on the size of the account or the duration of the omission. Timelines for submitting a late or amended report also differ among countries. For instance, Hong Kong, Singapore, and Switzerland each have their own deadlines and fine structures, and not all of them offer the same voluntary disclosure options. Checking the local rules is critical because the consequences of a late submission can be more severe if no disclosure is made.

## Compliance Recommendations

To reduce risk, regular reviews of all financial accounts held abroad should be part of an ongoing compliance routine. When an omission is found, acting quickly and following the official correction path is generally the safest approach. Engaging a tax professional who understands CRS obligations in the relevant jurisdictions can help ensure that the correction is complete and timely.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What should I do if I find an omitted offshore account after filing CRS?**

Review the original filing, prepare the missing account details, and submit a correction through the appropriate procedure in the relevant jurisdiction.

**Are there penalties for late CRS reporting?**

Penalties vary by country, so it is necessary to check the local rules for the amount and whether a voluntary disclosure programme applies.

**Can I use a voluntary disclosure programme to correct an omission?**

Not all jurisdictions offer such a programme. The availability depends on the country where the filing was made.

**How long do I have to correct a CRS filing?**

Deadlines differ among jurisdictions. You should confirm the specific time limit with the tax authority that received the original report.
